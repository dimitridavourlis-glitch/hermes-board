WHEN WOULD IT SAY BUY OR SELL?

When the trend state beats the market's own drift by more than 8 points. Example: one ticker rose over the next two months 74% of the time when above its average — but 73% of the time regardless. A 1-point edge, so it stays blank. At 8-plus it reads BUY, or SELL if the state pointed the other way. Even then it is a lean worth investigating, not an instruction.

WHAT IS HOLD?

Every buy-and-sell pair costs fees and spread. That cost is fixed, but the move you are trying to catch grows the longer you wait. Trade faster than this and fees eat more than a tenth of the typical move, so your timing stops mattering.

Where strength is moving

Left to right: how each group has done against the whole board over three months. Bottom to top: whether that lead is still building or fading in the last month. Groups drift clockwise — improving, leading, weakening, lagging.

IS THIS REAL MONEY FLOW?

No. Nobody outside a custodian can see money move. This is built from prices: a group outperforming and accelerating is what buying pressure looks like from the outside — but it is also what a short squeeze, a scheduled rebalance, or plain noise looks like. Treat the top-right as "has been working", not as "money is arriving".

Before you act

This predicts nothing. It describes where prices are and what usually followed. Even a 60% hit rate went the other way four times in ten.

It sees nothing others can't. Every figure comes from public closing prices.

The concentration numbers are the useful ones — the only things here known rather than guessed.
