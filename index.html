WHEN WOULD IT SAY BUY OR SELL?

When the trend state beats the market's own drift by more than 8 points. Example: one ticker rose over the next two months 74% of the time when above its average — but 73% of the time regardless. A 1-point edge, so it stays blank. At 8-plus it reads BUY, or SELL if the state pointed the other way. Even then it is a lean worth investigating, not an instruction.

WHAT IS HOLD?

Every buy-and-sell pair costs fees and spread. That cost is fixed, but the move you are trying to catch grows the longer you wait. Trade faster than this and fees eat more than a tenth of the typical move, so your timing stops mattering.
