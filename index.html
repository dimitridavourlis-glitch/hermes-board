What does "together" mean?

It is how closely two things move in step, as a percentage. 100% means they move identically. 0% means knowing one tells you nothing about the other. Above roughly 70%, you are holding one position wearing several names.

Why does it say "no signal" on everything?

Take an example. One ticker rose over the next two months 74% of the time when it was above its average — which sounds strong. But it also rose 73% of the time regardless. So the rule added one point. That is noise, not information. Most trading advice quotes the 74% and never mentions the 73%.

What is "hold at least"?

Every buy-and-sell pair costs money in fees and spread. That cost is fixed, but the price move you are trying to capture grows the longer you wait. Below the number shown, fees eat more than a tenth of the typical move and your choice of when to buy stops mattering. It is a floor, not a suggestion.

Before you act on this

This does not predict anything. It describes where prices are now and what has usually followed in the past. The past is not a promise, and even a 60% historical hit rate went the other way four times in ten.

It cannot see anything others cannot. Every number comes from public closing prices available to everybody.

The most useful thing here is the concentration number, because it is the only one that is actually knowable rather than guessed.
