HOW CAN 36 BE 6?

Things that move in step are not separate bets. If ten coins all follow Bitcoin, owning all ten is close to owning one position three times over. The number counts how many genuinely different outcomes you are exposed to.

WHAT DOES THE % MEAN?

How closely things move in step. 100% is identical. 0% means one tells you nothing about the other. Above about 70%, you are holding one position under several names.

WHY IS EVERY SIGNAL BLANK?

One ticker rose over the next two months 74% of the time when it was above its average — which sounds strong. But it rose 73% of the time anyway. The rule added one point. That is noise. Most trading advice quotes the 74% and never mentions the 73%.

WHAT IS MIN HOLD?

Every buy-and-sell pair costs fees and spread. That cost is fixed, but the price move you are trying to catch grows the longer you wait. Trade faster than this and fees eat more than a tenth of the typical move, so your timing stops mattering. A floor, not a suggestion.
