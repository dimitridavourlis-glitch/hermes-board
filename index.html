EDGE is how much the trend state beats knowing nothing — the hit rate when above trend, minus the hit rate in all windows. Anything inside ±8 points is noise, because these windows overlap heavily. HOLD is the shortest horizon on which costs stay under 10% of the average move; below it the entry rule stops mattering and the broker takes the edge.

What this cannot tell you

  • Which trades to place. The edge column is how you would know, and it is empty. Nothing here was validated as a strategy, and the strategies that were — trend, momentum, reversal, low-vol, over 27 years — did not beat buy-and-hold after costs.
  • Anything about the next 60 days. A 60% historical hit rate means it went the other way four times in ten, and you do not learn which draw you are in until afterwards.
  • Anything the price has not already absorbed. Every figure is computed from public closes that everyone else can also see.
